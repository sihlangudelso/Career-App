Career guidance for schools

Help every Grade 9 learner choose subjects with confidence.

Iroli Career Pathway matches South African learners in Grades 9–12 to careers and Grade 10 subjects that fit who they are — and shows your teachers where guidance is needed most.

What your learners get

  • A personality assessment that matches their interests and strengths against 77 real careers across 15 fields.
  • A Grade 9 Subject Choice Assessment that weighs what they enjoy, how they work and their latest marks.
  • Real entry requirements — degree programmes, APS scores and a built-in APS calculator.
  • A four-page personal report, downloadable as a PDF, to talk through with a parent or teacher.

What your school gets

  • Class join codes with seat tracking for each class.
  • A Grade 9 cohort dashboard showing pathway trends and who needs guidance.
  • CSV export of cohort data for your own records.
  • Admin roles scoped to a teacher’s own class or the whole school.
